Model equations

M1: b′ = ρb(1−b) − γbv; u′ = δ(1−u); v′ = u − v M2A: b′ = ρb(1−b) − γbv; u′ = δ − (δ+η)u; v′ = u − v M2B: b′ = ρb(1−b) − γbv; u′ = δ(1−u) − ηu·b/(h+b); v′ = u·b/(h+b) − v (corrected: no η in v′) Threshold (DFE stable iff R₀ < 1): M1: R₀ = ρ/γ · M2A: R₀ = ρ(δ+η)/(γδ) · M2B: DFE always unstable
Note: γ ∝ α and η ∝ α, so moving η alone at fixed γ is not a pure change of the activation rate α; scale γ and η together for that. b = B/K, u = P₁/(Λ/μ₁), v = P₂/(αΛ/(μ₁μ₂)), τ = μ₂t.
